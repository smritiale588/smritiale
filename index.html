About me ↓

Design that works as well as it looks.

I'm a UI/UX designer who builds simple, user-centered digital experiences. I turn complex ideas into clear, functional interfaces that people understand at first glance. I started as a UI/UX intern at CODE IT in Dharan, where I built my foundation in design principles, tools, and real client work. Today my work covers product design, wireframing, prototyping, and design systems.

I'm now an Intern Supervisor and Tutor at CODE IT. I review my students' work, sharpen their design thinking, and keep every project consistent and user-centered. To me, good design is not only how something looks. It's how it works.

MyDesign Process

Step 1

Research

Understand the users, their goals, and the problem before drawing anything.

Step 2

Wireframe

Map user flows and structure the layout so the experience is clear and simple.

Step 3

Prototype

Build high-fidelity, interactive designs in Figma that look and behave like the real product.

Step 4

Test and refine

Run usability tests, gather feedback, and improve the design until it works for real people.
